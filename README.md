# Ricochet Radio

**Electronic music, curated through code.**

Ricochet Radio is an internet radio project that brings together playlist automation, AI-assisted curation, and electronic music. It grew out of my work as a producer and DJ, and an interest in building systems that make music discovery feel intentional.

[Listen to Ricochet Radio](https://soundcloud.com/ricochet-radio/essentials-by-ricochet-radio-ep003) · [More music by WILDROP](https://soundcloud.com/wildropmusic)

## Project focus

- Playlist generation and scheduling with Python.
- AI-assisted curation to help shape the flow of a listening session.
- Streaming infrastructure built around Icecast.

## My contribution

I built the playlist and scheduling workflows and brought together the curation and streaming components, connecting my interests in software and music.
